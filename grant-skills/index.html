Centre for Teaching, Learning and Technology

Finish holding a proposal

A self-paced course for NUS educators who want to study their own teaching. You do not end it with a certificate. You end it with a drafted Teaching Enhancement Grant application.

The whole TEG application is about 1,400 words of prose in six boxes. That is genuinely finishable between classes. Each module writes one box, and what you write appears in the document below as you go.

You already know your discipline and your students. What is new is the research craft: framing a teaching problem as a question, choosing a lens, measuring learning rather than satisfaction, and getting it past a panel.

Everything you write stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. You can download it as a text file at any time.
