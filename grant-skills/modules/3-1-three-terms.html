Module 3.1 of Part 3

Three terms people confuse

Theory, theoretical framework, conceptual framework. Three different jobs, three different sections of your paper.

About 25 minutes. Eight steps. Everything you write stays on this device.

Step 1

About 1 minute

Why this matters

Most rejected education papers have neither a theoretical nor a conceptual framework. They have a literature review wearing a framework's clothes. A reviewer will write "the manuscript lacks a conceptual framework", and the author, who spent a month on the literature, will not know what is being asked for. This module makes sure you do.

What you will be able to do

Sort a research artefact into theory, theoretical framework, or conceptual framework, and say why the difference changes what you write.

Step 2

About 2 minutes

Where you stand

Four questions. This is not a grade. It is here so that you and we can see what the next twenty minutes actually add. You will meet the same four questions again at step 7, and you will see the answers then.

For each one, say how sure you are. Confidence that does not match accuracy is the useful signal, and you only get it if you answer honestly now.

Step 3

About 5 minutes

The idea

A theory

A theory is a general explanation of how something works, portable beyond any one study. Cognitive load theory is a theory. It makes claims about working memory that hold whether you teach chemistry or surgery.

Memory hook: a theory travels.

A theoretical framework

A theoretical framework is the specific theory you adopt to structure one study, with its constructs and predicted relationships imported wholesale. You commit to the theory's claims, and your findings can support or strain them.

Memory hook: a theoretical framework is a theory you have signed up to.

A conceptual framework

A conceptual framework is the argument you build for why this study, in this setting, makes sense. It draws on theory, on empirical literature, and on your own knowledge of the context. Ravitch and Riggan put it well: the conceptual framework is an argument, not a summary.

Memory hook: a conceptual framework is your case for the study.

The practical consequence

A theoretical framework tells you which constructs to measure. A conceptual framework tells the reader why your study is worth funding and reading. Most rejected education papers have neither, and instead have a literature review wearing a framework's clothes.

Watch out

The word "framework" in a name decides nothing. Ask what the thing does. Does it explain a mechanism that travels (theory)? Have you adopted it with its constructs and a prediction (theoretical framework)? Is it your argument for this study here (conceptual framework)? Or does it only sort things into kinds (a classification, which is none of the three)?

Step 4

About 4 minutes

Worked example

One case, worked in full. Write your own answer first, then reveal. The writing is what makes this work, so do not skip it.

Step 5

About 6 minutes

Practice: the card sort

Twelve cards, four bins. The fourth bin matters: some of these are none of the three, and saying so is a claim you have to make on purpose, not a way out.

Step 6

About 5 minutes

Your study

Now your own project. What you write here is saved on this device and nowhere else, and it carries forward to the other modules. It is meant to end up in a proposal, so write it as you would write it there.

Not every study adopts a single theory. A study framed conceptually, drawing on several, is a proper study. Say so plainly in the second box if that is yours.

Step 7

About 2 minutes

Check again

The same four questions, in the same order. Answer them again, and say how sure you are again. This time you get the answers, your score before and after, and whether your confidence matched your accuracy.
