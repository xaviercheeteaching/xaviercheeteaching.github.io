Before you begin — a note from the teaching team

This feedback form is completely anonymous. We do not collect your name, your email, your student ID, or any device or login information. There is no way for the teaching team to trace any response back to an individual student.

Our goal is simple: we want to make PHM5013 better. To do that, we need to hear what genuinely worked, what didn't, and what frustrated you. Critical feedback is the most useful thing you can give us — please be candid.

Your honesty will not affect your grade, your standing in the course, or your relationship with any of the lecturers. The form is only opened after grades are finalised, and lecturers will only see aggregated and anonymised feedback used to improve future iterations of the course.

Please write what you really think. We promise to read every response carefully and use it constructively.
